About me

I am an Associate Teaching Professor at Johns Hopkins University with a passion for teaching and developing new ways to teach content to students. I have developed several courses and now am working on integrating AI into the classroom. Below are some modules I have recently created.

Interactive learning modules

AAS and Standard Addition

Atomic absorption spectroscopy: basic concepts, applications, comparison with UV-Vis and emission, interferences, line width, and solved standard-addition examples.

Open module Source

Cyclic Voltammetry Primer

A pre-lab module on electrochemistry: Nernst thermodynamics, reversible and irreversible electron transfer, scan-rate dependence, and a plot-based quiz.

Open module Source

Cyclohexane Conformations

Rotatable 3D chair and boat models, an animated ring flip with its energy diagram, A-values and 1,3-diaxial strain, and a conformer population calculator.

Open module Source

Interactive Group Theory

Lessons on molecular symmetry: point groups, character tables, normal modes, and IR and Raman selection rules.
